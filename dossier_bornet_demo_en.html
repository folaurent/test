🧊 F-Gas III

Since 01/01/2025, refrigerants with GWP ≥ 2500 (R404A…) are banned for maintenance. GWP ≥ 750 banned in 2032.

→ A massive wave of replacement / retrofit across all your clients. A golden re-engagement argument.

🏢 Tertiary Decree

Buildings > 1,000 m² (central kitchens, care homes, schools): −40% energy by 2030, annual OPERAT declaration.

→ Sell energy-efficient equipment + monitoring contracts.

🥗 EGalim

50% sustainable / 20% organic mandatory. Actual 2024 figures: 29.5% / 11.8%, only 24% compliant. End of plastic cookware.

→ School canteens are reinvesting (vegetable prep rooms, stainless steel, reheating equipment).
